How many events can I enter?

You can select up to three events overall, with a maximum of two track events and two field events. Throws count as field events.

Who can enter the 800m?

The 800m is available to male students only under the college’s current event rules.

Which divisions are there?

There are four competition divisions: Degree Boys, Degree Girls, PUC Boys and PUC Girls.

What do I need to register?

Have your full name, student ID or USN, course, year, competition category and event choices ready.

Can I register the same student ID twice?

Each student should submit one registration. If you need to change an entry, contact the college organisers.

Where and when should I report?

Sports Day is on Saturday, 17 October 2026, at Mangala Stadium, Mangalore. The reporting time will be announced by the organisers. Check the schedule before travelling.

Are demo scores official?

Only results confirmed and published by the organisers are official. This frontend preview does not contain live scores.
